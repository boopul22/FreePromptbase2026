# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who use AI tools (ChatGPT, Midjourney, Claude, Gemini) and want a working prompt right now. They arrive from search or social, scan the gallery, copy a prompt, and leave. Secondary: contributors who submit prompts and browse their saved/liked collections.

## Product Purpose

Free Prompt Base (freepromptbase.com) is a free, browsable directory of ready-to-use AI prompts. Success = a visitor finds and copies a useful prompt in under a minute, and comes back when they need the next one. Copy, paste, create.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, practical, fast. Warm indie charm (gold/amber accent, Rubik) over a utilitarian core. The interface should feel like a well-organized pinboard, not a marketplace.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI/SaaS template: gradient heroes, glassmorphism, purple-blue AI clichés.
- Cluttered ad-heavy prompt directories / link farms.
- Sterile corporate dashboards: gray, lifeless, enterprise.

## Design Principles

1. **Copy is the conversion** — every screen optimizes the path from "saw it" to "copied it".
2. **The covers are the content** — imagery leads; chrome stays quiet and serves the grid.
3. **Fast over fancy** — performance budget is a design constraint (CF images, lazy-loading, minimal JS).
4. **Warm utility** — one gold accent used sparingly on actions; never decoration for its own sake.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Recent work already enforces 24px tap targets (2.5.8), accessible names on icon buttons, and focus-visible outlines — keep that bar. Respect prefers-reduced-motion for hover/like animations.
