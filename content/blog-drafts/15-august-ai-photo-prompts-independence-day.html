Pro Tips: 15 August AI Photo Prompts

Always upload your reference photo first when the prompt asks for one, then paste the full prompt. A clear, front-facing image gives the generator the best chance of keeping facial details natural.

If the first result looks close but not clean, generate a few more versions. Keep the version with correct hands, realistic flag fabric, natural skin texture, and readable English or Hindi text.

For Tiranga scenes, check that saffron is at the top, green is at the bottom, and the Ashoka Chakra is navy blue and centred. Small flag mistakes can make an otherwise good image feel wrong.

Aspect Ratio: Keep the ratio written in each prompt. The 4:5 concepts work well for feed posts, while 9:16 is better for Stories, Reels covers, and WhatsApp status updates.
