## How PromptPlum actually wins

**98% of their India traffic lands on the homepage.** It is a single page optimized for
the *"photo editing prompt"* head cluster:

- Title: `PromptPlum – #1 AI Photo Editing Prompts Library`
- H1: `AI Photo Editing Prompts Gemini & ChatGPT`
- Structure: `/library/<category>/` (men, woman, couple, family, birthday, christmas…),
  `/prompt/<slug>/` detail pages with **preview images on every card**, plus a free tool
  (`/tools/gemini-watermark-remover/`) and `/packs/`.

Their library/category pages earn almost nothing (≤22 visits/mo each). It's a
homepage-only play — which means outranking one URL beats most of their traffic.

## The plan to outrank them

### Phase 1 — Claim the "photo editing prompt" cluster (Cluster E, new)

This is the market PromptPlum lives on, and it's exploding (18k+ vol head term grew
90x in a year). Attack order = lowest KD first:

1. **New page `/tag/photo-editing-prompt`** — primary: `photo editing prompt`
   (720 vol, KD 18, rising). Secondary on-page: `ai photo prompt` (540, KD 17),
   `prompt for photo editing`. This is winnable at our authority level *now*.
2. **New page `/tag/chatgpt-photo-editing-prompt`** — primary:
   `chatgpt photo editing prompts` (18,100 vol, KD 74). Long game, but the volume
   justifies planting the flag early while the SERP is still young.
3. **New page `/tag/ai-image-prompt`** (or re-target `/category/images`) — primary:
   `ai image prompts` (US: 720 vol, KD 9; IN: 3,200 vol, KD 69). Secondary:
   `ai picture prompts` (KD 8), `image prompt`, `prompt image`. The US KD 9/8 terms
   are the fastest possible wins we have anywhere.
4. Homepage title/H1: keep `free ai prompts` as primary but work
   "photo editing prompts for Gemini & ChatGPT" into the subtitle/first paragraph —
   the homepage collects the head-cluster long tail the way theirs does.

### Phase 2 — Match their engagement signals (product work)

- **Preview image on every prompt card** (home grid + tag pages). Their cards all show
  the generated result; image-led SERPs (`images` feature on every cluster keyword)
  and user dwell time both favor this. Likely our single highest-leverage product change.
- **Ship one free tool** to earn links/utility queries, mirroring their watermark
  remover — e.g. a "Gemini prompt generator" or photo-prompt randomizer at `/tools/…`.
- Category browse by people-type (man / woman / couple / family / birthday) — we
  already have couple pages; add the missing person-type tags. These match how India
  users actually search.

### Phase 3 — Out-link them (the bar is 7 referring domains)

Their entire authority is ~7 refdomains (5 dofollow). Getting **10–15 real dofollow
refdomains** likely flips the authority comparison:

- Submit to AI-tool directories (theailibrary.co, toolify-class sites, alternativeto).
- Creator collabs: we already have creator tag pages (razz suman, ghaus editz, etc.) —
  ask those creators to link "get my prompts here" from their bios/descriptions.
- Pinterest/Instagram prompt-preview pins linking to tag pages (image-led niche).
- Our current 41 nofollow spam refdomains (.top/.sbs/seo-high-ranking.shop) are
  referral spam — ignore, don't build on them; disavow only if GSC shows a problem.
