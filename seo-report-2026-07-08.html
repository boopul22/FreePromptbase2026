6 Priority action plan

The site is built well but has no authority and no measurement of real traffic. Fix those two things and the strong on-page work starts converting into rankings.

Priority 1 · Highest impact

Build real domain authority

DA 2/100 is the ceiling on everything else. Earn a handful of genuine dofollow links: submit to AI-tool directories (Futurepedia, There's An AI For That, Toolify), get listed in "free AI prompt" roundups, share flagship pages in relevant Reddit/Discord/community threads, and pitch 2–3 guest posts. Even 5–10 quality links would move the needle dramatically at this level.

Priority 2 · Convert on-page into rankings

Get the 50 strategic pages indexed & internally linked

None of your target pages rank yet. Add prominent internal links from the homepage and high-traffic prompt pages to the flagship /nano-banana-prompt and the gemini ai photo prompt copy paste page. Confirm each is indexed in GSC (URL Inspection), and deepen the thinnest articles. Internal linking is the fastest lever you fully control.

Priority 3 · Technical polish

Clear the crawl warnings

Resize/convert the 89 oversized images (LCP), repoint the 152 internal links that hit redirects to their final URLs, add aria-labels to the 152 icon links, and trim the 74 long titles + 64 long descriptions. All low-effort, all in your codebase.

Priority 4 · Measurement — do this first, actually

Connect Google Search Console so real growth is measurable

This report couldn't show your true 28-day clicks/impressions because GSC isn't wired in. Connect it (and ideally create an SE Ranking project with daily rank tracking for your 50 target keywords). Then a month from now you'll have a real growth chart of your traffic — not a 4-keyword third-party estimate.
